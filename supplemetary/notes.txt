PROJECT OVERVIEW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

The project aims to develop an AI system that classifies cancer from lung CT scans, with a particular focus on early cancer detection. The work uses the LIDC-IDRI dataset (expert-annotated lung CT scans with nodules) and involves:

1. A multi-stage classification pipeline (CNN/Vision Transformer based)
2. Uncertainty quantification (MC dropout, deep ensembles, conformal prediction)
3. Inference-time self-correction mechanisms
4. Concept-based explainability (TCAV, ACE)
5. Ordinal classification of the 1-5 malignancy scale

The intended outcome is a publishable journal article in a reputable, impact-factor journal.
